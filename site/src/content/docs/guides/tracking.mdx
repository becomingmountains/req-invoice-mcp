Ask in plain language. The assistant uses `list_invoices` with the right filters and gives exact totals per currency.

## Who owes me?

> Who still owes me money?

```txt
8 invoice(s) (unpaid).
You're owed 1,250 USDC across 4 invoices and 0.5 ETH across 1 invoice.
- UNPAID 500 USDC for Acme "logo design" (2026-11-03) id 01ed…
- UNPAID 250 USDC for Bob "website" (2026-11-10) id 0133…
…
```

Totals are exact, with no rounding, and cover every matching invoice, not just the ones shown.

## Filters

| You say | Filter used |
|---|---|
| "unpaid only" | `status: "unpaid"` |
| "what got paid?" | `status: "paid"` |
| "this month" / "last month" | `period: "this_month"` / `"last_month"` |
| "in the last 30 days" / "this year" | `period: "last_30_days"` / `"this_year"` |
| "older than 2 weeks" | `olderThanDays: 14` |
| "for Acme" | `who: "Acme"` |
| "who paid short?" | `status: "partly_paid"` |
| "did anyone overpay?" | `status: "overpaid"` |

They combine: *"What's still unpaid from more than two weeks ago?"*

## Wrong amounts

Payers sometimes send less or more than the invoice asked. Both are flagged.

> Did Acme pay?

```txt
Invoice 01ed…: PART-PAID (status: created).
Amount: 100 USDC.
Short payment: received 75 of 100 USDC, so 25 USDC is still owed.
```

In `list_invoices`, a part-paid invoice shows as `PART-PAID 100 USDC (75 received, 25 still owed)`, and only the 25
counts toward what you're owed. An overpaid one shows as `OVERPAID 50 USDC (5 USDC over)`, so you can refund or credit
the difference.

Payment amounts come from the invoice's payment history. If a payer paid in another currency (for example, ETH for a
USD invoice), differences under 0.5% are treated as exact, since conversion rates move. To keep lists fast, payment amounts are checked on up to
100 invoices per request; the summary says so when there are more.

## Chasing a late payer

> Who's more than two weeks late? Draft a friendly reminder for each, with their link.

The assistant lists them, gets each saved link with `get_payment_link` and drafts the messages. **You** send them; this
server never sends messages.

Totals read up to the latest 500 invoices per account.
