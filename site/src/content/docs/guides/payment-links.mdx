A payment link is a hosted page where the payer connects their wallet and pays. You don't need their address in advance.

## Setup

Set `REQUEST_DESTINATION_ID` to your dashboard **Route ID**. The route decides the **network**, the **token** and the **wallet** that gets paid.

## Use it

> Send me a payment link for 250 USDC for the logo work.

The assistant calls `create_payment_link` and replies with:

```txt
Payment link created for 250. Send this to the payer:
https://secure.request.network/01J…
Invoice ID: 01e273ec… (use check_invoice to see when it's paid).
```

Send the link however you like: email, chat or a QR code.

## Check payment

> Has the logo invoice been paid?

<Aside>
  The amount is in your route's token. For a different token or network, set up another route
  or use [custom invoices](/req-invoice-mcp/guides/custom-invoices/).
</Aside>
