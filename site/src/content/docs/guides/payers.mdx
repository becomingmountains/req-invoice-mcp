Paying never goes through this server. There are two ways to pay.

## Open the link

Most payers open the payment link, connect any browser wallet and confirm.

## Pay programmatically

An agent with its own wallet can ask for the transactions:

> What do I need to sign to pay invoice 01e273ec… from 0x9c…e1?

`get_payment_transactions` returns, in order:

1. A token **approval**, if the payment contract can't spend the token yet.
2. The **payment** itself, carrying the invoice's payment reference.

It also flags insufficient balance or gas. The agent's own wallet tooling signs and sends them.

<Aside type="caution" title="Automating payments">
  Use a separate wallet holding only a small budget, never your main wallet, and enforce spending
  limits in whatever signs the transactions. Always check the recipient and amount before signing.
</Aside>
